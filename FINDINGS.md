# Analysis Findings

## Dataset Summary

- Customers: **800**
- Overall churn rate: **24.5%**

## Churn by Contract Type

- Month-to-Month: **35.3%**
- One Year: **15.0%**
- Two Year: **10.3%**

## Other Observations

- AutoPay = No: **27.9%** churn
- AutoPay = Yes: **21.4%** churn
- Satisfaction score 1-2: **42.2%** churn
- Satisfaction score 4-5: **19.5%** churn
- 5+ support tickets: **33.9%** churn
- Tenure 0-6 months: **50.8%** churn
- Tenure over 36 months: **19.4%** churn

## Interpretation

Higher churn is concentrated among customers with shorter contracts, lower satisfaction, more support interactions, and shorter tenure. These relationships are descriptive patterns in this synthetic dataset and should not be treated as causal conclusions.
